# Gender Digital Divide in India

### An Analysis of NSSO CMST 80th Round Data (2025)

## Project Overview
This project examines gender differences in digital access, internet usage and digital capabilities across India using the National Sample Survey Office's Comprehensive Modular Survey on Telecom (CMST), 80th Round, conducted between January and March 2025.

The analysis covers 141,865 individuals across 36 states and union territories.

## Research Objectives
- Measure gender differences in mobile access, internet usage and digital skills.
- Examine variations across rural and urban areas and age groups.
- Identify state-level differences in the gender digital divide.
- Examine individual and household-level predictors of internet ability.

## Methodology
- Data cleaning and preparation
- Weighted descriptive statistical analysis
- State-wise and demographic comparisons
- Logistic regression with average marginal effects
- Rural and urban probit models examining mobile phone type and internet ability

## Tools & Techniques
Python | pandas | NumPy | Statistical Analysis | Logistic Regression | Probit Regression | Data Visualisation

## Key Findings
- Women had a 10.7 percentage-point disadvantage in internet ability compared with men in the weighted estimates.
- The gender gap in online banking ability was 17 percentage points.
- The internet ability gap was particularly wide among individuals aged 45–59.
- Household internet access had a positive average marginal effect of 35.7 percentage points in the logistic regression model.

## Limitations
The analysis makes assumptions about respondents excluded by survey skip logic. The probit analysis also has limitations arising from the relationship between device ownership and internet ability. Refer to the full report for details.
